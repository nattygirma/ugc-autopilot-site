UGC Autopilot

Schedule and publish your own short-form videos to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook from one dashboard.

What it does

  • You upload a video you made. UGC Autopilot edits it into a short-form version for each of your accounts, adding a hook caption and music.
  • Every video waits in a review queue before it posts. You can edit the caption, swap the music, re-render or cancel it.
  • At the time you chose, it publishes the video to the accounts you connected, using each platform's official API.
  • It shows each post's live link in the dashboard.

How TikTok is used

You sign in with TikTok (Login Kit) to connect your own account. UGC Autopilot then uses the Content Posting API to upload your videos and either post them to your profile or send them to your TikTok inbox, where you finish posting in the TikTok app. Before posting, it shows the privacy options TikTok allows for your account, and it labels AI-generated content when you turn that setting on.

Requested permissions: user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish.

Where it runs

UGC Autopilot is self-hosted. It runs on the owner's own computer and stores everything there. There is no public sign-up and no shared server.
