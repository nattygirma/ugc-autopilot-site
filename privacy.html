Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what information UGC Autopilot ("the app") uses, why, and how it is stored. The app is self-hosted: it runs on its owner's own computer, and the data described here is stored on that computer, not on a shared server.

1. Information the app collects

When you connect a TikTok account, the app receives the following from TikTok, only with your permission:

  • Basic profile info (user.info.basic): your TikTok open ID, display name, username and avatar, so the app can show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens, so the app can post on your behalf until you disconnect it.
  • Video upload and publishing (video.upload, video.publish): the app uploads videos you chose and posts them to your account or your TikTok inbox. Before posting, it reads the posting options TikTok allows for your account, such as available privacy levels and maximum video length.
  • Your video list (video.list): the app reads the link and basic stats (such as views and likes) of the videos it published, to show them in its dashboard.

The app collects similar information when you connect Instagram, YouTube or Facebook accounts. It does not collect your passwords, contacts, messages, location, or information about your followers.

2. How the information is used

The information is used only to connect your accounts, publish the videos you scheduled, and show you how those posts are doing. It is not sold, rented, or used for advertising, and it is not used to build profiles of you or anyone else.

3. How it is stored

All data is stored in a database on the owner's own computer. Account tokens are encrypted at rest. The app does not send your platform data to any server operated by the app's developer.

4. Sharing with third parties

Data is sent only to the services needed to do the job you asked for:

  • TikTok, Instagram/Facebook (Meta) and YouTube (Google), to publish your videos and read their stats through their official APIs.
  • Higgsfield, an AI video service, which processes the videos you upload in order to create the edited versions. Your TikTok account information is not sent to Higgsfield.

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy policy.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept until you remove it. You can delete it at any time:

  • Disconnect an account from the app's Accounts page, which deletes its stored tokens and profile info.
  • Revoke the app's access from TikTok at any time under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Email nattygirma27@gmail.com to ask for your data to be deleted. Requests are handled within 30 days.

Videos already published to a platform stay on that platform until you delete them there.

6. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18, and it does not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: nattygirma27@gmail.com.
